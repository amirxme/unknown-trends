About

UNKNOWN ranks live Google Trends across ten regions. A signal rises when it appears in more places, with more search activity, and higher on those lists.

50%Coverage. How many regions show it.
30%Volume. How much search activity it has.
20%Position. How high it sits in each list.

History is saved on a schedule. New signals stay marked new until a second snapshot exists.
