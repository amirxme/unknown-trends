WHAT UNKNOWN DOES

We track what people are starting to search for.

UNKNOWN monitors search activity across multiple regions and turns raw trend data into comparable signals. The goal is simple: identify what is moving before it becomes obvious.

GLOBAL SCORE

One signal. Multiple dimensions.

Global Score combines three components: coverage, volume and position. Together they describe how widely a topic is appearing, how significant its search activity is, and how strongly it is positioned within regional trend lists.

MOMENTUM

Movement matters more than a snapshot.

Momentum compares current signals with historical snapshots. Velocity measures the rate of change, while acceleration shows whether that movement itself is increasing or slowing.

CONFIDENCE

More history. More context.

Confidence reflects how much supporting data is available for a signal, including historical observations and regional coverage. It is an indicator of data strength, not a guarantee of future movement.

DATA

Built from live search trends.

UNKNOWN currently uses Google Trends data across tracked regions. Snapshots are stored over time so that changes can be measured instead of relying on a single moment.
